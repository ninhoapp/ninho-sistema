This Privacy Policy explains how Mailson Valerio de Oliveira, based in Brazil, with its address at Rua Elizeu Prada, 113, Limeira/SP, Brasil ("Ninho," "we," "us"), collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects personal information in the Ninho app for iOS and Android and the related services (the "App"). It is part of our Terms of Use and uses the same definitions (Baby, Owner, Caregiver, AI Chat).

Ninho processes health information and children's information, including pregnancy information. Please read Sections 4 (sensitive data), 6 (what we don't do), 7 (advertising and measurement with Meta and Apple), and 14 (U.S. users) carefully.

KEY POINTS (this summary does not replace the full Policy):

  • We collect what you record about yourself and your Baby — routines, health, pregnancy, photos — to run the App and show that information to you and to the Caregivers you invite.
  • We do not sell personal information. We do not use health, pregnancy, or Baby data for advertising, and we never send records, photos, names, or messages to advertisers, Meta, or Google.
  • The App uses the Meta SDK and Apple Ads attribution to measure which ads bring new families. This sends Meta App usage events and technical device data (Section 7).
  • The AI Chat only works with your specific consent. Conversations are sent to Anthropic solely to generate answers, are not used to train models, and are automatically deleted after 180 days.
  • Nina, our AI support assistant, only works with your permission. What you write to her and some account details (never your Baby's records) are sent to Anthropic solely to generate answers, without training models. The conversation is kept for 12 months.
  • You can delete your account in the App and request a copy of your data from support.

1.1. Ninho is the controller of the personal information processed in the App.

1.2. Data Protection Officer (DPO): Mailson Valerio de Oliveira, email suporte@useninho.com.br. This is the channel to exercise your rights and to send privacy questions and complaints.

1.3. General support: suporte@useninho.com.br · useninho.com.br.

2. WHO THIS POLICY APPLIES TO

2.1. Adults who use the App: the Owner, who adds the Baby, and the Caregivers the Owner invites.

2.2. The Baby, whose information is entered by parents, guardians, or authorized Caregivers. The Baby does not use the App and has no account.

2.3. People invited as Caregivers, whose email address is provided by the person who invites them.

2.4. The App is designed for adults (18 or older) and is not directed to children.

3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

3.1. Account information: name, email, password (stored only in encrypted form by our authentication provider), profile photo (optional), language, time zone, units of measurement, notification preferences, your optional answer to "How did you hear about Ninho?", and the date and version of the Terms, this Policy, and the AI Chat consent you accepted.

3.2. Sign in with Apple or Google: if you choose it, we receive your name, email (or Apple's relay email), and an account identifier. We never receive your Apple or Google password.

3.3. Baby information: name, date of birth or due date (when added during pregnancy), sex (optional), photo (optional), and birth measurements.

3.4. Routine and health records: sleep, breastfeeding and feeding, diapers, baths, medications and doses, vaccines, allergies, appointments, weight, height and other measurements, events, free-text notes, and anything else you or Caregivers record. Also the estimates the App calculates from those records, such as the sleep prediction.

3.5. Photos and moments: images you upload, with date, caption, and milestone, and the albums and videos you generate.

3.6. Caregivers and invitations: the email of people you invite, each account's link and role for the Baby, and who created or changed each record.

3.7. AI Chat: your messages, the generated answers, the model and rules version that produced each answer, and any reports you submit. Dictated audio is not stored (Section 10).

3.8. Subscription: plan, period, start and end dates, subscription status, and transaction identifiers provided by the App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store card details.

3.9. Technical and usage information: IP address, date and time of access, device model and operating system, App version, push notification token, error logs, a change history of records (for security and auditing), and whether you opened the notifications and announcement emails we send.

3.10. Ad attribution information: if you installed the App on an iPhone, Apple's response about whether the install came from an Apple Ads ad and which campaign; and the data the Meta SDK collects on your device (Section 7).

3.11. What stays on your device only: the biometrics or passcode for the App lock (checked by your device's operating system and never sent to us), events you choose to copy to your device calendar, photos you save to your gallery, and a temporary copy of data so the App can work offline.

3.12. Sources: you; Caregivers of the same Baby; Apple and Google (sign-in and subscriptions); Apple (Apple Ads attribution); and your device (technical data). Meta does not send us information that identifies you individually.

3.13. Support (Nina): your messages, the generated answers, the model and rules version that produced each answer, questions forwarded to our team (summary, category, and answer), and the date you allowed Nina to be used.

4. SENSITIVE INFORMATION: HEALTH, PREGNANCY, AND CHILDREN

4.1. Health information — including pregnancy information such as the due date — is sensitive personal information. We process it only for the purposes in this Policy, with your specific and prominent consent, given when you accept the Terms and this Policy and when you record that information, or where the law allows it without consent (for example, to comply with a legal obligation or to exercise legal rights).

4.2. Baby information is children's information and is always processed in the child's best interest: to organize the child's routine and care. The person who enters it represents that they are the parent or legal guardian, or are authorized by one, and that person is the one who consents to the processing.

4.3. We do not use health, pregnancy, or Baby information for advertising, to build marketing profiles, or for automated decisions that affect your rights, and we do not share it with advertisers. It is only seen by you, by Caregivers you invite, and by the service providers in Section 8, to the extent necessary to run the App.

4.4. In the U.S., this information may be "consumer health data" under state laws; Section 14 contains the specific notice and your rights.

5. HOW WE USE INFORMATION AND OUR LEGAL BASES

  • Creating and maintaining your account, storing and displaying records, photos, and moments, sharing the Baby with Caregivers, calculating estimates, generating albums and videos, and sending the reminders you turn on — performance of our contract with you and, for sensitive and children's information, your specific consent.
  • AI Chat and voice dictation — specific consent, requested before first use (Section 10).
  • Support through Nina — your consent, requested on screen before your first message, to send the conversation to Anthropic; and performance of contract to answer your questions and resolve account and subscription issues (Section 10).
  • Processing and verifying subscriptions, the free trial, and access shared among Caregivers — performance of contract.
  • Messages about using the App (push or email), which may take into account the Baby's age or due date, for example to tell you about features for that stage or to congratulate you on the birth — consent, which you can withdraw by turning off notifications or using the unsubscribe link in the email.
  • Announcements, news, and offers — legitimate interest or consent where the law requires it, always with an opt-out. These announcements do not use health information.
  • Measuring which ads bring new families (Section 7) — legitimate interest, without using health or Baby information, with the controls in Section 7.5.
  • Security, fraud and abuse prevention, usage limits, and protection against unauthorized access — legitimate interest and exercise of legal rights.
  • Reviewing reported AI Chat answers to fix failures — legitimate interest and user safety.
  • Keeping access logs and complying with legal, regulatory, and tax obligations — legal obligation.
  • Defending Ninho in legal proceedings and complaints — exercise of legal rights.
  • Improving the App — only with aggregated or de-identified information.

6. WHAT WE DON'T DO

We do not sell personal information. We do not rent or trade it for money or other benefit.

We do not send Baby records, health or pregnancy information, photos, names, emails, or AI Chat messages to Meta, Google, advertisers, or data brokers.

We do not use your Baby's image or information in advertising without your prior, specific, written consent.

Our contracted artificial intelligence providers do not use your information to train their models.

7. ADVERTISING, MEASUREMENT, AND ATTRIBUTION (META AND APPLE ADS)

7.1. We promote Ninho with ads on Instagram, Facebook, and the App Store. To learn which ads work, we use the tools below. They do not receive health, pregnancy, or Baby information, but they do allow those platforms to know that someone installed and uses Ninho — a baby and pregnancy tracking app.

7.2. Meta SDK. The App includes the software development kit (SDK) of Meta Platforms, Inc. It automatically sends Meta: (a) usage events, such as installing and opening the App and, on Android, purchases made through Google Play; (b) a new subscription event, with the plan, period, and price; and (c) technical device data, such as model, operating system, App version, language, time zone, and IP address. On Android, it also sends Google's advertising ID if it is enabled on your device. On iPhone, Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) would only be sent if you allowed tracking through the system prompt (App Tracking Transparency); the App does not currently show that prompt, so the IDFA is not shared.

7.3. Meta uses this data to measure and optimize our ads and, under its own terms and Privacy Policy, may also process it for its own purposes, including by associating it with your Facebook or Instagram account. Learn more at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

7.4. Apple Ads. On iPhone, the App obtains an attribution token from Apple (AdServices framework) and sends it to Apple, which responds whether the install came from an Apple Ads ad and which campaign, ad group, keyword, and ad, with the click date and country. We store that response linked to your account to learn which campaigns bring subscribers. This does not use the IDFA, does not require tracking permission, and does not send Apple any of your in-App information.

7.5. Your controls: (a) iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising; (b) Android: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID; (c) Meta: in your account settings, under "Your activity off Meta technologies" and ad preferences; (d) you may ask us, by emailing the DPO, to delete the Apple Ads attribution and "How did you hear about Ninho?" answer linked to your account. U.S. users may also opt out of sharing for advertising (Section 14).

8.3. Third parties acting on their own behalf: Apple and Google, as the App Stores that sell subscriptions, and Meta, as described in Section 7. They follow their own privacy policies.

8.4. Authorities: when required by law or a valid legal order, or to protect someone's life or safety. Where permitted, we will notify you.

8.5. Corporate transactions: in a merger, acquisition, or sale of the business, information may be transferred to the successor, which will be bound by this Policy. We will notify you in advance.

9. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Ninho is based in Brazil, and several providers in Section 8 are located in or store data outside Brazil, mainly in the United States. These transfers rely on contractual data protection clauses and other safeguards required by Brazilian law (LGPD, Article 33) and, where applicable, on your specific consent. By using the App, you understand that your information may be processed in Brazil and the United States.

10. AI CHAT, VOICE DICTATION, AND SUPPORT (NINA)

10.1. The AI Chat is optional and is only enabled after your specific consent, which we record with the date and the version of the text you accepted.

10.2. When you send a message, we send Anthropic PBC the message and the context needed to answer: the Baby's name and age, recent routine and health records, and earlier messages in the conversation. Nothing is sent if you do not use the AI Chat. Anthropic processes this information only to generate the answer, does not use it to train models, and may retain it briefly for safety and abuse prevention under its contract.

10.3. Conversations are kept for 180 days and then automatically deleted, together with any reports about them. Caregivers of the same Baby can also see the conversations about that Baby.

10.4. If you report an answer, an authorized member of the Ninho team may read the reported answer and the conversation it appears in, solely to understand and fix the failure. Without a report, our team does not read your conversations, except to comply with the law or investigate abuse.

10.5. Voice dictation: if you record audio, it is sent to our transcription provider (Groq or OpenAI) with the Baby's first name, solely to be converted into text. Ninho does not store the audio, on the device or on our servers. The text appears for you to review before sending.

10.6. You may withdraw your consent at any time by emailing the DPO; the AI Chat will no longer be available to your account, and you may ask us to delete conversations before the retention period ends.

10.7. In-App support is provided by Nina, an artificial intelligence assistant. Before your first message, we ask for your permission to send the conversation to Anthropic. If you prefer not to, support remains available at suporte@useninho.com.br.

10.8. When you write to Nina, we send Anthropic PBC your message, earlier messages in the conversation, and these account details: first name, partially hidden email, plan and free trial or subscription status, the name of the person sharing their plan with you (if any), account creation date, language, device operating system and App version, how many Babies and Caregivers the account has, the status of invitations you sent (with the invitee's email partially hidden), and summaries of questions you previously forwarded to our team. We do not send your Baby's records, photos, name, or age. Anthropic processes this information only to generate the answer, does not use it to train models, and may retain it briefly for safety and abuse prevention under its contract. Please do not write your Baby's health information in support; if you do, it will be handled the same way, and Nina will refer health questions to your pediatrician.

10.9. Anything Nina cannot answer becomes a question for the Ninho team, which may read the conversation to answer it. The answer arrives in the same conversation, with a notification. Team answers may be turned into generic questions and answers, with no information that identifies you, for Nina to use with other people.

10.10. Conversations with Nina and questions forwarded to our team are kept for 12 months and then automatically deleted, or sooner if you delete your account. Caregivers cannot see your conversation with Nina. You may withdraw your permission and ask us to delete the conversation by emailing the DPO.

11. HOW LONG WE KEEP INFORMATION

  • Account, Babies, records, photos, and moments: for as long as the account exists. When you delete your account, we permanently delete everything; shared Babies remain with the other Caregivers, and only your link is removed.
  • AI Chat conversations and reports: 180 days.
  • Support (Nina) conversations and questions forwarded to our team: 12 months.
  • Dictated audio: not stored.
  • App access logs (IP address, date, and time): 6 months, as required by Brazil's Internet Civil Framework (Law 12,965/2014, Article 15), or longer if a court orders it.
  • Subscription and transaction data: for the period required by tax and consumer laws and to defend against claims (generally up to 5 years).
  • Apple Ads attribution and "How did you hear about Ninho?": for as long as the account exists, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Backups: deleted in the normal rotation cycle, within 30 days.
  • Data sent to Meta: subject to Meta's retention policy.

12. SECURITY AND INCIDENTS

12.1. We use encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, per-Baby access control in the database (each account can only read data for the Babies it is linked to), private storage for moment photos accessed through temporary links, random non-listable addresses for profile photos, an optional biometric App lock, and restricted staff access.

12.2. No system is completely secure. If a security incident may create a relevant risk or harm, we will notify Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) and affected individuals within the regulatory deadlines and, in the U.S., affected individuals, the Federal Trade Commission, and other authorities, as required by the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule and state laws.

13. YOUR RIGHTS (BRAZIL)

13.1. Under the LGPD (Article 18), you may request: confirmation that we process your information; access; correction; anonymization, blocking, or deletion of unnecessary or unlawfully processed information; portability; deletion of information processed based on consent; information about whom we share it with; information about the option not to consent and its consequences; withdrawal of consent; and review of automated decisions.

13.2. How to exercise them: (a) you can correct and delete records in the App; (b) to delete your account: Settings > Delete account; (c) for a copy of your data and other requests: email the DPO from your account email. There is no automatic export inside the App yet; we will send the copy in a readable electronic format within 15 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

13.3. Information about a shared Baby belongs to the care tracking of all its Caregivers. Requests about that information are handled taking into account the rights of the other guardians and the child's best interest.

13.4. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out, but it may prevent the use of features that depend on it (for example, recording health information or the AI Chat).

13.5. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the ANPD or to consumer protection agencies.
